AeriVoice 0.2.5

A chosen microphone starts
If the microphone picked in Settings > Dictation had a different sample rate or channel count from the Mac's default devices, every dictation ended at once with an error. AeriVoice now opens the microphone in its own format.

Fallback to the default microphone
If a chosen microphone is connected but still cannot be started, the dictation runs on the system default and the notch shows "Using the default microphone".

Paste into a new Mail message
Dictating into Mail's compose body copied the text instead of pasting it. It now pastes.

Cleanup failures stay visible
When cleanup fails and the raw transcript is pasted, the notch shows the warning for two seconds instead of the check mark.

Clipboard restored sooner in an empty Notes or Messages field
A paste into an empty field is now verified, so your clipboard comes back once the text has landed.
