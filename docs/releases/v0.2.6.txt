AeriVoice 0.2.6

Update before January 15, 2027 if you use Soniox
From that date Soniox refuses the way earlier versions of AeriVoice send your API key, and dictation through Soniox stops working on them.

Soniox v6
Soniox transcription now uses its new v6 model, named Soniox Realtime v6 in Settings. Your key, Dictionary and spoken language carry over.

Turn AI cleanup off
Switch off AI Cleanup > AI cleanup in Settings to paste the transcript as transcribed. Nothing goes to a cleanup provider, and no cleanup key is needed. Cleanup stays on unless you change it.

What looks different with Soniox v6
Prices are written as spoken, such as "15 dollars and 20 cents" instead of "$15.20". Hyphens in codes appear only when you say them. Spoken punctuation and commands such as "period" and "new line" reach cleanup as words, and AI cleanup applies them; how reliably depends on the style and model. With cleanup off they stay as words.

Text survives a failed transcription
If the connection drops or a provider doesn't finish in time, the text already shown is copied to the clipboard and the notch says so.

A modifier-only shortcut starts only when you mean it
Using its key for an ordinary combination ends the dictation that press started, and pressing Escape while holding the shortcut no longer starts another one.

Paste works on every keyboard layout
Dvorak and Turkish-F now paste instead of sending Command-K or Command-C.

A press too early says so
Pressing the shortcut while the last dictation is finishing plays the error sound and shows "Still finishing—press again when done".

Key repeats stay out of your app
Holding a shortcut's key after releasing its modifier no longer types that key into the focused app.

Microphones that change during start-up
A microphone that stops as it starts, as opening a Bluetooth headset's can, is now replaced, and a restart just after the start cue keeps your speech.

Multi-input audio interfaces are heard
A microphone on an interface with an unknown channel layout was converted to silence. All inputs are now averaged into mono.

Cartesia no longer passes off provisional text as final
If the connection closed before AeriVoice could send the end of the dictation, the partial transcript was pasted as though it were complete. It now counts as a failure, and the text already shown is copied instead.
