# AeriVoice 0.2.6 — Soniox v6 and steadier dictation

AeriVoice 0.2.6 moves Soniox transcription to its new v6 model, keeps Soniox working past its January 2027 sign-in change, and fixes a set of shortcut, paste and microphone bugs.

**Update before January 15, 2027 if you use Soniox.** From that date Soniox refuses the way earlier versions of AeriVoice send your API key, and dictation through Soniox stops working on them.

### What's new

- **Soniox v6:** Soniox transcription now uses `stt-rt-v6`, which replaces v5. Nothing to set up; your key, Dictionary and spoken language carry over.
- **Soniox sign-in:** your Soniox key now travels with the connection instead of inside the first message.

### What looks different with Soniox v6

- **Prices are written as spoken:** "15 dollars and 20 cents" instead of "$15.20". AI cleanup sometimes turns them back into symbols, but not reliably.
- **Hyphens in codes appear only when you say them:** "NP38BX" instead of "NP-38BX".
- **Spoken punctuation and commands reach cleanup as words:** Soniox now writes "period", "new line" and "scratch that" as words, and AI cleanup applies them. Results depend on the style and model: Compose is the most reliable, and Faithful often keeps them as written. If cleanup fails and the raw transcript is pasted, you will see the words themselves.

### What's fixed

- **Text survives a failed transcription:** if the connection drops or a provider doesn't finish in time, the text already shown is copied to the clipboard and the notch says "Transcription was cut off—copied what was heard".
- **A modifier-only shortcut starts only when you mean it:** using its key for an ordinary combination, such as fn-Delete or Right ⌘-C, ends the dictation that press started, without a message or cue. Pressing Escape while still holding the shortcut no longer starts another one.
- **Paste works on every keyboard layout:** Dvorak and Turkish-F now paste instead of sending ⌘K or ⌘C.
- **A press too early says so:** pressing the shortcut while the last dictation is still refining or pasting plays the error sound and shows "Still finishing—press again when done", so your next sentence isn't spoken into nothing.
- **Key repeats stay out of your app:** holding a shortcut's key after releasing its modifier no longer types that key into the focused app.
- **Microphones that change during start-up:** a microphone that stops as it starts, as opening a Bluetooth headset's can, is now replaced instead of leaving the recording stopped, and a restart just after the start cue keeps your speech.
- **Multi-input audio interfaces are heard:** a microphone on an interface or aggregate device with an unknown channel layout was converted to silence. All inputs are now averaged into mono, so one active input among several sounds quieter but is transcribed.
- **Cartesia no longer passes off provisional text as final:** if the connection closed before AeriVoice could send the end of the dictation, the partial transcript was pasted as though it were complete. It now counts as a failure, and the text already shown is copied instead.

### Install or upgrade

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or newer**. Use **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or download the DMG below, open it, and drag AeriVoice into Applications. Existing settings and Keychain credentials are retained.

The DMG is Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and stapled. A SHA-256 checksum and debugging symbols are attached.
