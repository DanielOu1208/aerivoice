# AeriVoice 0.2.3 — Cartesia transcription and tighter recording edges

AeriVoice 0.2.3 adds Cartesia as a transcription provider, keeps everything you say up to the moment you release the shortcut, and starts the microphone sooner when sounds are off.

### What's new

- **Cartesia transcription:** choose **Cartesia Ink 2** or **Cartesia Ink Preview** in **Settings → Dictation** and connect a Cartesia API key. Ink 2 is Cartesia's stable model; Ink Preview is its preview model and can change without notice. Both transcribe English, French, Hindi, Japanese, and Spanish only. Your Dictionary is sent as hints, up to 100 terms totaling 1,200 characters (fewer in non-Latin scripts). Terms that don't fit stay saved and are listed in Dictation settings. See [Providers and models](../PROVIDERS.md#cartesia).
- **Recording ends exactly at release:** audio reaches AeriVoice in blocks of about a tenth of a second, and the block in progress when you released the shortcut could be dropped, cutting off the end of the last word. AeriVoice now waits for that block and keeps everything up to the release. Recording no longer continues for 50 ms afterwards.
- **Faster start with sounds off:** the microphone starts at the key press instead of after AeriVoice's key and permission checks. After you cancel a dictation, the next one starts on a microphone engine that is already prepared.
- **Recommended model:** the Dictation model picker marks Soniox as the recommended cloud model.

### What's fixed

- **Microphone lost while starting:** if your input device went away in the first moments of a dictation, AeriVoice could record silence and report "No speech detected". It now reports the microphone error.

### Install or upgrade

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or newer**. Use **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or download the DMG below, open it, and drag AeriVoice into Applications. Existing settings and Keychain credentials are retained.

The DMG is Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and stapled. A SHA-256 checksum and debugging symbols are attached.
