# AeriVoice v0.1.0-beta.8 — Local dictation and Offline mode

Draft release notes. The installer has not been built or published yet.

- **Dictate on your Mac:** choose Apple Speech or the recommended NVIDIA Nemotron model for English. Apple Speech can reuse installed language support; Nemotron requires a 611 MB download. Local accuracy may be lower than cloud models.
- **Offline mode:** enable it from General or the menu bar once a local model is set up. It bypasses AI cleanup and requires no cloud keys. Your normal provider choices are preserved when switching back online.
- **Simpler setup:** local models are prominent in onboarding. One model selector covers cloud and local options, while Providers owns downloads, language setup, status, and removal. Interrupted NVIDIA downloads can be resumed or removed after reopening the app.
- **More shortcut control:** choose Hybrid, Toggle, or Hold, and optionally distinguish left and right Command and Option keys. Trackpad activation is not included.
- **Settings and recovery fixes:** a larger minimum settings window, clearer download failures, safer Apple language reservations, and onboarding recovery when a model or permission becomes unavailable.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26+. Selecting a local model alone does not disable cloud cleanup; enable Offline mode to keep dictation entirely local. macOS manages Apple language assets and may maintain or retry previously requested downloads independently.

## Updating

The final installer, checksum, and validation summary will be added after release packaging. Existing keys and preferences are intended to remain intact; clean-install and upgrade acceptance are tracked in [the readiness checklist](v0.1.0-beta.8-readiness.md).
