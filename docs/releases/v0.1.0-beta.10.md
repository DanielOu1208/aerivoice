# AeriVoice v0.1.0-beta.10 — Grok transcription and clearer provider icons

- **Grok transcription:** connect an xAI API key in Providers, then choose Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 in Dictation.
- **Dictionary hints:** send up to 100 eligible terms to Grok. Unsupported entries stay saved, with exclusions visible in Settings; recognition hints do not guarantee exact spelling.
- **Faster Grok startup and catch-up:** reuse a prepared connection for up to 30 seconds and drain buffered audio at up to 1.35× real time without changing the audio samples. In our small production catch-up comparison, median cold-stop delay fell from 585 to 342 ms; this is not a guarantee for every recording or network.
- **More reliable connection handling:** fresh and prepared Grok sessions share one transport implementation, with regression coverage for duplicate finish requests, cancellation, and replacement sessions.
- **Clearer provider icons:** corrected SpaceX clearspace, OpenRouter contrast, and Cerebras geometry in Settings and the README.

Grok requires xAI API credits. Mixed-language recognition and AI cleanup can still make mistakes; review important text. Catch-up pacing has been tested empirically but is not an explicit xAI support guarantee. See [Grok validation](../GROK-VALIDATION.md) for benchmark methods and limitations.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26+. Updates are manual. Existing settings and saved credentials are retained.

## Updating

Download `AeriVoice-v0.1.0-beta.10-arm64.dmg` and its matching `.sha256` file. Verify the checksum, open the DMG, quit AeriVoice, and replace the app in Applications.

Build: `2026092002`. Debug symbols and a source/build receipt accompany the installer.
