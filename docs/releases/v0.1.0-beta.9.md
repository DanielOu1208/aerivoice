# AeriVoice v0.1.0-beta.9 — Cleanup styles and a new icon

The signed and notarized installer is ready in the draft release; publication is pending.

- **Compose (Experimental):** format dictated lists and paragraphs, apply clear spoken corrections, and refine your writing within the current recording.
- **Custom (Experimental):** choose Custom in the cleanup-style dropdown to reveal your instructions. Set tone, spelling, translation, or formatting on top of Polished cleanup. Instructions apply only in Custom and stay saved when you switch styles. Empty Custom uses Polished cleanup.
- **Clearer style choices:** Faithful, Polished, Compose, and Custom each have a short description. Polished remains the default, with improved cleanup of fillers and phrasing.
- **New microphone mark:** refreshed app, menu-bar, settings-sidebar, and README icons. The menu-bar mark turns orange while recording.

Compose and Custom can miss edits or change meaning. Review important text. Higher reasoning may help where supported, but adds latency and has not been verified to improve reliability in our benchmarks. Your reasoning setting is unchanged. Offline mode skips all AI cleanup.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26+. Updates are manual.
