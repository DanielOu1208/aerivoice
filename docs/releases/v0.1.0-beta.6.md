# AeriVoice v0.1.0-beta.6 — Earlier connections and clipboard restoration

This update starts transcription connections earlier, reduces the remaining
audio queue when using Meta, and can restore your previous clipboard after a
verified paste.

## What changed

- **Earlier Soniox connection:** connection setup starts alongside dictation
  startup, including the optional sound cue. Recording can proceed while the
  connection finishes; captured audio is buffered and sent in order.
- **Faster Meta audio catch-up:** the existing bounded catch-up rate now remains
  active while queued audio remains, including small final fragments. Normal
  pacing resumes when the queue empties.
- **Restore your previous clipboard:** this setting is enabled by default.
  AeriVoice restores an in-memory backup only after the destination exposes the
  exact expected text edit and the clipboard remains unchanged. When verification
  or backup is unavailable, your dictation stays copied.
- **Prepare recording at launch:** after onboarding and microphone permission,
  AeriVoice prepares the audio engine without starting microphone capture.
  Startup rechecks the current audio route and replaces stale preparation.
- **Optional local diagnostics:** diagnostics are off by default. Opting in adds
  startup and resource information to local, content-free records. Existing
  history is pruned when the app starts even if recording diagnostics is disabled.

Provider and network delays vary. Sound cues remain an existing user preference;
this release does not change their default. You can disable them in Settings
to remove the cue's startup pause.

## Clipboard behavior

The green check mark means AeriVoice sent Paste; it does not itself confirm that
the destination accepted the text or that restoration completed. Restoration
uses separate text, selection, focus, and clipboard checks. Copies made while
dictation is finishing are preserved. Unsupported destinations keep the dictation
on the clipboard. Change this behavior with **Restore clipboard after dictation**
in Settings.

## Updating from beta.5

Existing provider keys and preferences use the same storage locations. macOS
may request permission when needed. Updates remain manual.

1. Quit AeriVoice.
2. Download `AeriVoice-v0.1.0-beta.6-arm64.dmg` and its `.sha256` file.
3. Verify the download with
   `shasum -a 256 -c AeriVoice-v0.1.0-beta.6-arm64.dmg.sha256`.
4. Open the DMG, drag AeriVoice into Applications, and choose **Replace**.
5. Reopen AeriVoice from Applications.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later, your own transcription
and cleanup provider accounts, and Microphone and Accessibility permissions.
