# AeriVoice 0.2.1 — Smoother notch, cleaner microphone capture, and steadier connections

AeriVoice 0.2.1 makes dictation feel smoother and loses fewer words at the start and end of a recording.

### What's new

- **Smoother notch:** the recording notch animates without resizing its window each frame. In our 30-cycle test, detected hitches fell from 95 to 54 and the longest from 33 to 17 ms.
- **Recording starts sooner:** with sounds on, the microphone starts while the start sound plays, so speech is kept from about 200 ms after the key press instead of about 475 ms.
- **Fewer clipped endings:** capture continues 50 ms after you stop, and the stop sound plays only after the microphone closes.
- **Microphone choice:** pick an input in **Settings → Dictation → Microphone**. System default stays the default; a disconnected choice falls back to it.
- **Device changes mid-dictation:** switching inputs no longer silences the rest of a recording. If the new input cannot start, AeriVoice finishes with what it already captured.
- **Clearer input:** multichannel microphones are mixed down instead of using only the first channel, and a light filter removes low rumble such as fans and desk bumps.
- **Clipboard restore delay:** **Settings → General → Clipboard** lets you choose how long to wait before restoring your clipboard when an app cannot confirm the paste.
- **Faster first requests:** Groq and OpenRouter cleanup connections are prepared when you start dictating, and a prepared Grok connection is kept for up to five minutes.
- **Retiring OpenRouter models:** retired models are hidden, retiring ones are labeled, and Settings warns if your selected model is retiring.
- **Reliability:** literal `<think>` text in dictation is preserved with Cerebras, incomplete provider responses fall back to your raw transcript, and a pending clipboard restoration can no longer be cancelled early before an update restart.

Timing figures come from local tests on one Mac and are not guarantees for every device or network. See [Optimization release notes](../OPTIMIZATION-RELEASE.md) for methods and limitations.

### Install or upgrade

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or newer**. Use **Settings → General → Check for Updates**, or download the DMG below, open it, and drag AeriVoice into Applications. Existing settings and Keychain credentials are retained.

The DMG is Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and stapled. A SHA-256 checksum and debugging symbols are attached.
