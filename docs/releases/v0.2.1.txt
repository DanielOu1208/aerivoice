AeriVoice 0.2.1

Smoother notch
The recording notch opens and closes without resizing its window every frame, so animation hitches are roughly halved.

Fewer clipped words
With sounds on, the microphone starts while the start sound plays, so speech is kept from about 200 ms after you press the shortcut instead of about half a second. Capture also continues briefly after you stop, and the stop sound plays only after the microphone closes.

Choose your microphone
Pick an input in Settings > Dictation > Microphone. System default remains the default, and a disconnected choice falls back to it.

Steadier recordings
Switching inputs mid-dictation no longer silences the rest of a recording. Multichannel microphones are mixed down, and a light filter removes low rumble such as fans and desk bumps.

Clipboard restore delay
Choose how long AeriVoice waits before restoring your clipboard when an app cannot confirm the paste, in Settings > General > Clipboard.

Faster first requests
Groq and OpenRouter cleanup connections are prepared as you start dictating, and a prepared Grok connection is kept for up to five minutes.

Retiring OpenRouter models
Retired models are hidden, retiring ones are labeled, and Settings warns you if your selected model is retiring.

Reliability
Literal think tags in dictation are preserved with Cerebras, incomplete provider responses fall back to your raw transcript, and pending clipboard restoration is handled more reliably around update restarts.
