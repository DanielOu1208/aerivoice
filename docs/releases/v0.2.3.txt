AeriVoice 0.2.3

Cartesia transcription
Choose Cartesia Ink 2 or Cartesia Ink Preview in Settings > Dictation and connect a Cartesia API key. Both models transcribe English, French, Hindi, Japanese, and Spanish only. Your Dictionary is sent as hints, up to 100 terms.

Recording ends exactly at release
AeriVoice keeps everything you said up to the moment you release the shortcut. Before, the last tenth of a second could be dropped, cutting off the end of the last word.

Faster start with sounds off
The microphone starts at the key press. After you cancel a dictation, the next one starts on a microphone engine that is already prepared.

Recommended model
The Dictation model picker marks Soniox as the recommended cloud model.

Microphone lost while starting
If your input device goes away as a dictation starts, AeriVoice now reports the microphone error instead of "No speech detected".
