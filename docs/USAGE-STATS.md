# Usage stats

Open **Settings → Stats** for words dictated, average dictation WPM, completed sessions, and recording time over 7 days, 30 days, or all time.

- Counts use the final output after cleanup, including text copied to the clipboard.
- Average WPM divides total output words by total recording minutes, including pauses but excluding processing time. Cleanup and language affect counts, so rates are not comparable across languages.
- Stats stay on your Mac and never retain audio or transcripts.
- Manage collection or clear totals under **Privacy & Data → Usage stats**. Turning collection off keeps saved totals.
