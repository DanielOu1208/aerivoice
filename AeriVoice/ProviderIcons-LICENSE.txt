SpaceX provider mark
===================

Assets: Assets.xcassets/ProviderSpaceX.imageset/icon.png and
        ../docs/images/providers/spacex.png
Source: https://www.spacex.com/assets/favicon-spacex.ico
Referenced by: https://www.spacex.com/
Retrieved: September 19, 2026

The official 48 x 48 favicon supplies the white SpaceX mark on black. Its
48 x 48 representation is scaled proportionally into a 96 x 96 black tile
with 9 pixels of clear space on every side. Only the tile corners are rounded;
the complete original mark remains visible. The same PNG is used in the docs.
The mark is used to identify the Grok (xAI) provider, not to imply endorsement.
SpaceX and its mark belong to their respective owner. This third-party brand
asset is not covered by AeriVoice's or LobeHub's MIT license; no permissive
license is asserted for it.
